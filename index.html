Security PoC — DOM XSS on account-opening-uat.hsbc.com.hk

One click demonstrates arbitrary JavaScript execution in the origin of account-opening-uat.hsbc.com.hk via a javascript: URL rendered into an <iframe src> by the #/webview route.

On click: a popup opens at #/?channel=M (passes the UAT gate), then after ~2.5s the opener performs a same-document fragment navigation to #/webview?url=javascript:alert(document.domain). When the gate's ~5s timeout re-renders and mounts the route, the iframe executes and an alert() shows the victim host's own domain — proving same-origin XSS. Total time to popup: ~5s.
